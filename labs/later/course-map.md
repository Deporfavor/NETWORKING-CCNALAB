> **About CCNA v2.0.** Cisco has published a new CCNA (still exam 200-301) that starts on **3 February 2027**. v1.1 can be taken until **2 February 2027**. The routing, switching and IP services skills in labs 1-10 carry straight across. v2.0 puts more weight on hands-on automation (for example *using* Ansible rather than just describing it), so Lab 20 will be written for v2.0.
